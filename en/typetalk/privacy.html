Privacy policy: TypeTalk

Effective 22 October 2026.

TypeTalk does not collect your personal data, does not send it to us and does not share it with anyone. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Who makes the app

TypeTalk is developed by Shoxi Labs (Kristijan Šorn). For any privacy questions, write to shoxilabs.dev@gmail.com.

Data on your device

Your saved phrases and settings such as font, colors and language are stored only on your device. We don't send them anywhere and have no access to them. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, the system may back up the app's data to your Google account. You control this backup in your device settings, and we have no access to it.

Permissions

TypeTalk does not request any special permissions, such as the microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Children

TypeTalk does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data, we hold no data about you to show, correct or delete. If you have a question, contact us at the address above.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll publish the new version on this page with a new date.
