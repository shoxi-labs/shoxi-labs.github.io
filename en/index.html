TypeTalk

Coming soon to Google Play

Type a message and show it to the other person in large letters, face to face.

  • Large text across the whole screen
  • Flip the screen 180° to face the person across from you
  • Read the typed text aloud
  • A sign across the whole landscape screen, with an attention signal
  • Quick phrases, with the option to save and edit your own
  • Optional phrase categories and subcategories, e.g. Transport → Taxi, Bus
  • Text size via slider or pinch zoom
  • Adjustable colors and text alignment, hide the buttons you don't use and choose their order
  • The screen stays on, and your last message stays after reopening
  • Built-in user guide
