TypeTalk

Coming soon to Google Play

Type a message and show it to the other person in large letters, face to face. For deaf and hard of hearing people, and for anyone who finds it easier to write than to speak or listen.

  • Free
  • No ads
  • Works offline
  • For Android
  • Large text facing the person across the table
  • Read aloud and a full-screen sign
  • Quick phrases, optionally in categories
